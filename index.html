FAQ

Common questions.

How often should I have my aircon cleaned?

For daily home use, every 3 to 6 months. Units in dusty areas, near roads, or in restaurants and kitchens need cleaning every 2 to 3 months.

My aircon is running but not cooling. What's wrong?

The usual causes are a clogged filter or coil, low refrigerant from a leak, or a failed capacitor on the outdoor unit. Book a diagnosis and we'll tell you which one before any repair.

Do you service inverter units?

Yes. We clean, troubleshoot and repair inverter split-types, including reading error codes from the indoor display.

Why is water dripping from my indoor unit?

Usually a blocked drain line or a dirty coil that freezes and then melts. A general cleaning with a drain flush fixes most cases.

How do I pay?

Cash, GCash or bank transfer after the job is finished and tested.
